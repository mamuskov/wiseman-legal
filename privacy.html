WiseMan – Mafia Simulator

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you use WiseMan – Mafia Simulator (“the App”, “we”).

In short: you do not need an account and your game progress stays on your device. However, advertising, in-app purchases and analytics rely on third-party services that process device-level identifiers.

1. Account and identity

The App does not ask you to create an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or postal address.

The App does not collect location data and never requests the device location permission.

2. Data kept on your device

Your game progress (character, stats, inventory, settings, language) is stored only in your device’s local storage. It is not sent to our servers — we do not operate one. Deleting the App deletes this data.

3. Advertising

The App is free and shows ads. Which network is used depends on your device:

  • iOS and Android devices with Google Play services: Google AdMob
  • Devices without Google Play services (Huawei / AppGallery): Unity LevelPlay (Unity Ads / ironSource)

To deliver and measure ads, these networks process device-level information: the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, the advertising ID / OAID on Android), IP address, operating system version, device model and network type.

Ad consent

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent form (UMP) is shown on first launch and your choice is recorded.
  • On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt is also shown.

Personalized ads are requested only if both of these are granted. If you decline either one, you will still see ads, but they are requested as non-personalized. You can withdraw consent at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).

Details: Google · Unity / ironSource

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by your device’s own store (App Store, Google Play or AppGallery). Your payment details stay with the store; no card, billing or address information reaches us.

To validate purchases and let you restore them on another device we use RevenueCat. What RevenueCat receives is an anonymous app-generated user ID and the transaction itself (product, date, status). RevenueCat privacy policy

5. Analytics

On iOS and in the Google Play (Android) build, the App uses Firebase Analytics to record in-game events (for example completing a chapter) so we can see which screens are used and where the game gets stuck.

The AppGallery (Huawei) build contains no analytics at all. Firebase is not included in that build because Huawei devices have no Google Play services; not a single event is sent.

6. Text you write yourself

At one point the game asks which feature you would like to see and opens a text box. What you type there (up to 100 characters) reaches us as an analytics event — that is, it leaves your device. Please do not type personal information (name, email, phone) into that box; we only expect requests and suggestions. On the AppGallery (Huawei) build, which has no analytics, this box sends nothing anywhere.

7. International transfers

The third parties above (Google, Unity, RevenueCat) run their servers outside Türkiye, mainly in the European Union and the United States. When you use the advertising and purchase features, this technical data is transferred abroad.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for adults and is rated 17+ on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your choices

Because we hold no account or record about you, there is no personal data of yours for us to delete. What you can do:

  • Delete the App — all game data on the device goes with it.
  • Reset your advertising identifier or withdraw tracking consent in device settings.
  • Ask a question or object — use the address below.

10. Changes

This policy is updated whenever a new service is added to the App. The date at the top shows the last update.
