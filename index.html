Frequently asked

How do in-app purchases work?
Purchases are processed by your device’s own store — the App Store, Google Play or Huawei AppGallery. You can restore them at any time from inside the app, on any device signed in to the same store account.

I paid but did not receive my items.
First try Restore Purchases in the app. If that does not help, email us with the store receipt and we will sort it out.

How do I manage ad tracking?
iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.
Android: Settings › Google › Ads.
Huawei: Settings › Privacy › Ads & privacy.

Where is my game saved?
Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud save, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

Which languages are supported?
14 languages, including English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
